Dian Nuswantoro University (UDINUS) is one of the most prominent private universities in Central Java, Indonesia, located in the heart of Semarang City. Established with a strong commitment to advancing education, research, and innovation, UDINUS has become widely recognized for its excellence in information technology, entrepreneurship, and creative industries. The university offers a wide range of study programs across several faculties, including Informatics Engineering, Economics and Business, Health Sciences, Communication and Visual Arts, and Humanities.

What makes UDINUS stand out is its dedication to combining academic learning with practical, industry-based experience. Students are encouraged to apply their knowledge through internships, research projects, and community service programs, enabling them to develop not only technical skills but also leadership, creativity, and social awareness. The Faculty of Informatics Engineering is one of the university’s strongest assets, consistently producing talented graduates in software engineering, data science, artificial intelligence, and cybersecurity.

Moreover, UDINUS actively collaborates with national and international institutions to enhance research, innovation, and global exposure. The university also provides a supportive and modern learning environment, equipped with advanced facilities, digital classrooms, and student innovation centers. Through its vision to become a world-class university that integrates technology, creativity, and entrepreneurship, UDINUS continues to empower young generations to become agents of change who contribute to society and compete in the global arena.
